What it is

  • A model of a model: it copies the ngspice simulation, which copies the KiCad schematic. It is not fitted to a real TB-303 or x0xb0x.
  • The four-rung diode ladder, Q12, Q21 and the whole feedback loop are solved together each sample, with the coupling capacitors, the output tap, the MEG, Env Mod, the accent circuit with C13, and the VCA.
  • The VCO is the study's ideal saw and the square shaper as ngspice drew it. The Cutoff taper is the study's assumption, 20 % at half rotation.
  • Left out: noise, hum, drift, the headphone amp and pair mismatch.
